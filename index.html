3Words and phrases

9 minutes · no translation, only simple explanations

Words to know

reluctant adj.

= not wanting to do something, and slow to do it. Similar: unwilling.
He was reluctant to go on tour, but he agreed for his family.

resent verb

= feel angry and bitter about something unfair.
She resented her boss for taking the credit for her work.

exploit verb

= use a person unfairly to get something for yourself.
Some companies exploit young workers: long hours, little pay.

overwhelmed adj.

= feeling that you have too much to handle.
With work, kids and a house move, she felt overwhelmed.

perfectionist noun

= a person who wants everything to be perfect.
He is a perfectionist: he filmed the video ten times.

vulnerable adj.

= easy to hurt, in body or in feelings.
After the accident, he felt vulnerable.

relentless adj.

= never stopping, never easy.
The pressure was relentless: shows, interviews, flights.

legacy noun

= what a person leaves behind, and people remember.
Her legacy is a school that still helps 500 children.

Phrases and idioms

Look at the tricky ones: they sound like one thing, but mean another.

I'm so sick of it

= I'm fed up. I can't take it anymore.
I'm so sick of this traffic. I'm taking the train.

do my own thing

= live or work my way, without anyone controlling me.
I love my team, but sometimes I just want to do my own thing.

the man of the hour

= the person everyone is talking about right now.
He scored the winning goal. He was the man of the hour.

Here we go (again)

= the same problem is starting again. Often a little annoyed.
My neighbour started drilling at 7 a.m. Here we go again.

walk on eggshells

= be very careful around someone, so they don't get angry.
It's not about eggs!
With a strict father, the kids walked on eggshells.

call the shots

= be the one who decides.
It's not about shooting!
At work my boss calls the shots, but I plan my day.

put up with

= accept something annoying and not complain.
I can't put up with this noise anymore.

stand up to someone

= be brave and say "no" to a strong person.
She finally stood up to her boss and asked for a day off.

sleep on it

= wait until tomorrow before you decide.
You don't have to sleep on anything.
Don't answer now. Sleep on it.

steal the show

= get all the attention, be the best part of an event.
Nobody is stealing a TV!
The little dog stole the show at the wedding.

Pick three for this week:

Ask the student to guess the meaning from the example first. In the scene you can hear: exploiting, I'm so sick of it, I just want to do my own thing, man of the hour, Here we go. The other words and idioms fit the film's themes: control, pressure, family and courage.
